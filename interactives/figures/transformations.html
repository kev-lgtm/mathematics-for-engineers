A matrix moves the plane

Drag the two arrow tips (the columns of A) and watch the unit square, the letter F and the whole grid follow.

The matrix A

Try this
  1. Drag the blue tip to (2, 0) and the orange tip to (0, 2). By what factor did the area grow? Compare with det A.
  2. Make the two columns point along the same line. What happens to the square? Is there any way back?
  3. Swap the two columns (blue to (0, 1), orange to (1, 0)). The F is mirrored. What is the sign of the determinant?
  4. Choose Rotation and set 90°. Where do i and j go? Compare with the matrix in the text.
  5. Drag the green point x and follow the path: Ax is always x1(first column) + x2(second column).
