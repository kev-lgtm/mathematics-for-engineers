A picture is a matrix

Each pixel is one entry (0 = black, 9 = white). Brightening a photo multiplies the matrix by a number; blending two photos adds two matrices. Click a pixel to follow it through the calculation.

Try this
  1. Choose Brighten (k = 2). Which pixels reach white and stop changing? What detail is lost?
  2. Choose Blend. Click a pixel and check by hand that rij = ½aij + ½bij.
  3. Choose Difference (A − B). Why do many entries become 0?
  4. Change a single entry of A in the matrix. How many entries of R change?
  5. Set k = 0 and l = 1. What is R? Which matrix does R equal when k = 1 and l = 0?
