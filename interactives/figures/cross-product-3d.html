Cross product, area and volume in 3D

Choose a vector and set its components with the sliders. Rotate the scene by dragging it (or focus it and use the arrow keys). u × v is perpendicular to both; its length is the area of the shaded parallelogram.

Try this
  1. Start: u = (2,1,0), v = (1,2,0) gives u×v = (0,0,3), area 3, as in the book.
  2. Swap the roles with show v × u: the arrow flips (right-hand rule).
  3. Make v a multiple of u: the parallelogram collapses and the cross product is 0.
  4. Switch on the box and lift a into the plane of u, v: the volume drops to 0, so the three vectors are coplanar.
