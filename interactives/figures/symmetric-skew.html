Every square matrix is symmetric + skew

Edit the entries of A. The matrix S = ½(A + AT) averages each entry with its mirror image, K = ½(A − AT) keeps what is left over. Point at an entry to see its mirror partner.

Drag the tip of x. Blue + vermilion arrows (head to tail) always end exactly at the purple arrow.

Try this
  1. Point at the entry a12 of A. Which entries of AT, S and K light up? How are s12 and s21 related, and k12 and k21?
  2. Press Make A symmetric. What is K? Press Make A skew. What is S?
  3. Why is the diagonal of K always zero?
  4. Drag x. What angle does Kx make with x, always? (Think of xTKx.)
  5. Switch to 3 × 3 and decompose a matrix of your own.
