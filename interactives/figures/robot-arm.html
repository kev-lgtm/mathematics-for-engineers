A two-link robot arm

Link 1 is turned by θ1, link 2 by a further θ2. The tip is p = R(θ1)([L1, 0]T + R(θ2)[L2, 0]T). Drag the elbow or the tip.

Try this
  1. Press Text example (L1 = L2 = 1, 30°, 60°). Check that the tip is (√3/2, 3/2) ≈ (0.87, 1.50).
  2. Press Exercise and compare the tip with your own calculation (L1 = 2, L2 = 1, 60°, −30°).
  3. Turn on Show p = v1 + v2. Change only θ2. Which of the two vectors turns, and which stays fixed?
  4. Turn on the reachable region and drag the tip outwards. What happens at the outer edge? Which value of θ2 is that?
  5. Keep the tip fixed and use the flip button. Two different joint settings give the same tip: why does the matrix formula allow that?
