Matrix multiplication, term by term

Each entry is a row of A times a column of B: cij = ∑k aikbkj. Click any entry of A or B to change it.

Try this
  1. Press Step and follow one entry of C from start to finish. How many products does it need?
  2. Choose the 1×3 times 3×1 preset. What is the size of the result, and what is it called?
  3. Switch to Falk’s scheme. Why does the highlighted row of A always meet the highlighted column of B exactly at the entry being computed?
  4. Tick “Make the inner sizes disagree”. Which two numbers must be equal for the product to exist?
  5. Type a 1 in one entry of A and 0 elsewhere. What does the product do to B?
