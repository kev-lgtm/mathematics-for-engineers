The order of two maps matters

Build two machines A and B. The top row applies B first and then A (the product AB); the bottom row does it the other way round (BA).

Try this
  1. Start from the text example: A is a quarter turn, B is a shear. Compare the two final pictures. Which entries of AB and BA differ?
  2. Make both machines rotations. Do the final pictures agree? Why would you expect that?
  3. Make one machine a scaling with equal factors in x and y. Does it commute with everything? Why?
  4. Try a horizontal shear and a vertical shear. Then two horizontal shears.
  5. Make B a projection. Can you still tell which matrix produced a flat picture?
