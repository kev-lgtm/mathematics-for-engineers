Gauss–Jordan elimination, step by step experimental

Row operations turn A into the identity. Done to [ A | b ] they solve A x = b; done to [ A | I ] they produce A−1. All numbers are exact fractions.

Try this
  1. Choose Find the inverse and press Next until the end. Check the result by multiplying: the figure does it for you.
  2. Choose the preset Needs a row swap. Why can the first step not be a division?
  3. Choose Singular. Where does the elimination get stuck, and what does that say about det A?
  4. Switch to Solve Ax = b and choose Singular with the two different right-hand sides. One has no solution and one has infinitely many. How do you read that from the last rows?
  5. Type your own matrix. Before pressing Next, predict which pivot comes first and which multiples will be subtracted.
