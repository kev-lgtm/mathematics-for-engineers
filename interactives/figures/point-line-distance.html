Distance from a point to a line, and its mirror image

Drag the two blue points to move the line Ax + By + C = 0 and the black point Q. The perpendicular is the shortest way from Q to the line; walk the same distance again to reach the image Q′.

Try this
  1. Start position: the line 3x − 4y + 5 = 0 and Q = (2, 1) give d = 7/5 = 1.4, as in the book.
  2. Slide the orange point R along the line: |QR| is smallest exactly at the foot of the perpendicular.
  3. Move Q across the line: the signed value δ changes sign, the distance does not.
  4. Put Q on the line: d = 0 and Q′ = Q.
