Dot product, projection and reflection

Drag the tips of u and v (or focus a tip and use the arrow keys). The green arrow is the projection of u onto the line of v; the dashed remainder is always perpendicular to v.

Try this
  1. Start position: u = (3, 2), v = (2, 1) gives u·v = 8 and the projection (3.2, 1.6), as in the book.
  2. Turn u until the dot product is 0: the projection shrinks to a point and the angle reads 90°.
  3. Swing u past 90°: the dot product turns negative and the projection points backwards.
  4. Switch on the reflection: the mirror image of u in the line of v is 2·proj − u, and it always has the same length as u.
