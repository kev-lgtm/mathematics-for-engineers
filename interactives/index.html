Nine figures of the book made interactive. Each one is a single page that also works on its own: use Preview here to try it on this page, or Open full page for the full-size version. Handles can be dragged, or focused with Tab and moved with the arrow keys (Shift = larger steps).

Figure 1A matrix moves the plane

Drag the images of i and j and watch the whole plane follow. Presets: rotation, shear, reflection, projection, singular.

Try: Make the determinant zero. What happens to the grid?
