Interactive tools

Live figures for the Analytic Geometry notes (Part 2, chapters 4 to 8): vectors, lines, conics, planes and surfaces. Nothing to install and no internet needed: drag the points, move the sliders, and turn the 3D pictures with the mouse or finger.

Experimental. These interactive figures are new and still being developed. Some things may not work perfectly yet, and more figures and features are coming. If something looks wrong, please let the author know.

49 figures, 21 of them in 3D. Each card names the related section of the notes. Each figure is a single page that also works on its own: use Preview here to try it on this page, or Open full page for the full-size version. Points can be dragged, or focused with Tab and moved with the arrow keys (Shift = larger steps). Most figures also appear inside the notes, next to the figure they belong to (the card says where). A 3D picture turns when you drag it (or focus it and press the arrow keys; Home resets the view). On a phone, drag outside a 3D picture to scroll the page. The matching tools for Part 1 are on the Linear Algebra page.
